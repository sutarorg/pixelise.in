`docs/blog-keyword-map.md`; the method and its limits are in
`docs/research/blog-keyword-research.md`.

**There is no volume, difficulty or CPC data in this repository.** No keyword tool was
available while building it. Anything numeric would have been fabricated.

## Cannibalisation rules

- `/` owns "AI product photography" and the brand-modified variants. Articles must not try
  to rank for the bare head term in their titles.
- `/pricing` owns pricing queries. Articles link to it rather than repeating the pack table.
- `/blog/ai-product-photography-ecommerce-guide` owns the "what is AI product photography"
  question. Other articles link to it instead of re-explaining the category.
- The Shopify and Amazon guides are the only pages targeting platform requirements. Do not
  add platform-specific sections to other articles.

## What is deliberately not targeted

- "Best AI product photography tools" (unverifiable competitor claims)
- "Product photography near me" / any local service intent (not offered)
- Subscription or "free trial" phrasing (pricing is one-time packs only)
- Competitor brand names, except in general comparison framing with no claims
