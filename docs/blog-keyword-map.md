# Blog keyword map

Operational map for the 12 launch articles. Companion documents:
`docs/research/blog-keyword-research.md` (method and honest limitations),
`docs/seo/internal-linking-map.md` (link graph), `docs/blog-sources.md` (citations).

**No search-volume, difficulty or CPC data is recorded here.** No keyword tool was
available in the build environment, so any number of that kind would have been invented.
What follows is intent, audience and target phrasing — the parts that can be decided
without a tool.

## Category assignment rationale

| Category | Articles | Why it exists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Product Photography | 1 | The product's category, treated with its limits stated |
| Ecommerce Photography | 2, 3 | Platform-specific searches with high compliance stakes |
| Product Photography Guides | 4–11 | The craft cluster; supports every other page with practical detail |
| Advertising & Marketing | 12 | Connects imagery to paid media without performance claims |

## Rules for future articles

1. One primary intent per URL. If two articles would target the same phrasing, merge them.
2. No numeric claims without a primary source. If a platform's spec cannot be verified
   in this environment, the article points the reader to the platform's own page.
3. Every article carries an organisational byline (`Pixelise Editorial Team`), a real
   publication date, and at least two sibling links plus one commercial link.
4. No "updated for 2026" freshness edits. `dateModified` changes only when content does.
5. Do not publish comparison or "best tool" pages until competitor claims can be
   verified; do not publish case studies until there are customers who consented.
